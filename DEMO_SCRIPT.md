# VittSetu Demo Script

1. Open Home and say: "VittSetu starts as a citizen-friendly bridge into NSFDC credit, not as another dense portal."  
   Gap shown: radical accessibility.

2. Tap "Find a Scheme" and use the demo persona: business purpose, project cost Rs 95,000, annual income Rs 3,20,000, self-employed, no disability.  
   Gap shown: NSFDC-specific decision support.

3. Select the Micro Finance Scheme and open the Calculator. Point out that the calculator inherits the selected scheme and uses scheme limits, own contribution, moratorium, and repayment tenure.  
   Gap shown: NSFDC-specific decision support.

4. Open Partner Locator. Select Micro Finance and Pune. Show that Maharashtra Scheduled Castes Development Corporation ranks above nearer or lower-capacity options.  
   Gap shown: capacity-aware routing.

5. Toggle the anonymized success signals on the map. Say: "These are aggregate signals only; no beneficiary name, address, or amount is exposed."  
   Gap shown: trust without privacy leakage.

6. Open the partner report card. Narrate typical turnaround, success rate, capacity status, and verified partner badge.  
   Gap shown: capacity-aware routing.

7. Start an application from the partner sheet. On the tracker, draw a demo e-Sign and submit. Advance the stage once from demo controls.  
   Gap shown: end-to-end journey.

8. Raise a grievance from the tracker. Explain that the citizen sees a case ID and service deadline instead of being left uncertain.  
   Gap shown: end-to-end journey.

9. Switch the role selector to "Partner staff" and open Partner Dashboard. Change capacity from high to medium, then return to Partner Locator to show the same partner health signal has changed.  
   Gap shown: capacity-aware routing with a real feedback loop.

10. Switch the role selector to "NSFDC policy" and open Policy Dashboard. Show aggregate demand, drop-off, and partner performance without exposing individual applicants.  
    Gap shown: policy visibility without personal data exposure.

11. Open VittGyan, complete "Avoid Middlemen", and show the streak and badge shelf.  
    Gap shown: radical accessibility and safety education.

12. Open the accessibility settings, increase text size, enable high contrast, and use a read-aloud button. Then open "Feature-phone help" from Home to show the missed-call IVR concept.  
    Gap shown: radical accessibility.

Reset between runs: use the development-only "Reset demo data" button in the header.
